Copyright & Permissions

Why VerseNest posts original examples and how we handle rights.

Original examples only

The starter lyric pages were newly written for this project. They are creative examples, not official lyrics for a recording by a commercial performer. We do not knowingly publish copyrighted third-party song lyrics without permission.

Commercial song lyrics

Most lyrics from released songs are protected by copyright. Attribution or a link to the singer is not a replacement for permission. Before adding a third-party song, obtain any needed permission or license from the appropriate owner or licensing provider.

Requesting reuse

To quote or republish substantial amounts of content, request permission through the website owner’s verified contact information. The owner should set their actual permissions policy and contact address before launch.

Reporting concerns

If you believe content on the published website violates your rights, contact the website owner with the URL and a description of the issue. The owner must supply an active contact channel before launch.
