Copyright, Licensing & Rights Policy

Our commitment to protecting artist intellectual property, transparent rights classification, and rapid takedown compliance.

1. Our Principles on Song Lyrics & Composition Rights

VerseNest is an independent, educational Sri Lankan music discovery platform. We believe in honoring the creative rights of songwriters, composers, lyricists, and music publishers. We strictly forbid unauthorized scraping or reproduction of copyrighted song lyrics.

2. Six-Tiered Rights Classification System

Every song in our catalogue is classified into one of six legal copyright states:

  • Original Owned: Original creative works written and owned directly by VerseNest Originals.
  • Permission Granted: Lyrics published with documented, verified permission from the copyright owner or lyricist.
  • Public Domain: Traditional folk songs and verified historic works whose copyright protection has expired.
  • Licensed Attribution: Works published under Creative Commons or documented statutory licenses with strict attribution.
  • Rights Unknown (Lyrics Unavailable): Songs where publisher permission has not yet been verified. For these titles, only verified metadata (singer, composer, year) and official streaming links are shown. Full lyrics are withheld.
  • Permission Rejected / Revoked: Works where rights holders have requested exclusion or permission was declined. Lyrics are permanently blocked.

3. Notice and Takedown Procedure

If you are a songwriter, composer, lyricist, recording artist, or authorized publishing representative and believe any content appears without appropriate permission, we provide a rapid, 1-click administrative takedown workflow.

Direct Takedown Submission: Please submit your notice via our Contact Portal or email copyright@versenest.org with:
1. Title and Artist of the composition.
2. Specific URL on VerseNest.
3. Evidence of ownership or agency.
Action will be taken within 24–48 business hours.

4. Public Music Metadata Sources

Non-copyrightable factual bibliographic data (song titles, artist names, release years, genres, and composer credits) are indexed for educational and archival discovery using permissible public databases including MusicBrainz, following API terms of service and rate limitations.
