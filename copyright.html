Copyright, Licensing & Takedown Policy

Our commitment to intellectual property protection, verified songwriting credits, and zero unauthorized lyrics reproduction.

Commitment to Artist Rights

VerseNest Originals is built on the principle of deep respect for musical creators, lyricists, composers, and recording artists. We do not engage in unauthorized scraping or mass unlicensed publication of copyrighted lyrics.

Our Three-Tier Rights Framework

  • Original Owned Content: Creative lyrics written specifically as original demo texts by our contributors for educational and songwriting analysis.
  • Authorized / Permission Granted: Third-party lyrics published strictly with verifiable authorization or established public-domain standing.
  • Rights Unknown / Restricted: Where copyright ownership resides with commercial publishers and explicit digital text distribution rights have not been secured, full lyrics are strictly shielded. In these cases, we present verified metadata, educational commentary, and direct links to official streaming platforms.

Notice & Takedown Mechanism

If you are a copyright owner, lyricist, composer, or publisher and believe any content on VerseNest infringes your intellectual property, please submit an expedited takedown notice through any of the following channels:

  1. Click the Notice & Takedown Portal button on the target song's detail page for immediate registration in our admin review queue.
  2. Email our legal team at rights@versenest.org with the target song ID and proof of ownership.

All credible notices result in immediate removal of lyrics text within 24 hours.

Google AdSense & Monetization Policy

Pages that do not feature authorized lyrics do not serve advertisements. VerseNest adheres strictly to publisher policies against low-value scraped content, ensuring all indexable pages offer unique educational or original creative merit.
