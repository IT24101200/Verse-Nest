Data & Privacy Standards

Privacy Policy

Transparency regarding user privacy, local storage usage, and third-party advertising disclosures.

Information We Collect and How It Is Used

VerseNest is committed to protecting visitor privacy. We do not require public user accounts, logins, or personal details to explore our music catalogue, read lyrics, or access songwriting guides.

Local Storage & Cookies

We use browser localStorage strictly for user preferences—specifically preserving your selected Dark or Light theme (versenest_theme) across page visits. We do not track users across third-party websites or sell personal data.

Hosting & Technical Logs

Standard HTTP web server request logs (including IP address, browser type, and timestamp) are processed transiently for server security, rate-limiting brute-force login attempts on administrative portals, and diagnosing system faults.

Advertising & Analytics Disclosure

This website is designed for compliance with Google AdSense terms and policies. When third-party advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve non-personalized or personalized ads subject to applicable privacy laws and consent frameworks.

Rights Holder Submissions

If you submit a copyright notice or takedown request through our portal, your submitted contact name, email address, and relationship are stored securely in our administrative database solely for verifying and executing your request.

Contact Regarding Privacy

For any questions or data requests, contact our privacy officer at privacy@versenest.org.
