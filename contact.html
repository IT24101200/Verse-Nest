Editorial Communications

Contact VerseNest

Connect with our editorial team with feedback, song metadata corrections, or rights inquiries.

Get in Touch with Our Team

We welcome feedback from listeners, musicians, lyricists, and educators. Whether you have suggested metadata corrections for an existing catalogue record or would like to submit lyrics authorization, our editorial desk is here to help.

Communication Channels

  • Editorial & Metadata Corrections
    contact@versenest.org

    For submitting verified recording credits, lyricist attributions, or release dates.

  • Copyright & Licensing Requests
    rights@versenest.org

    For copyright holders seeking authorized lyrics publication or expedited takedowns.

What to Include in Your Message

  • For metadata corrections, include the song title, artist, and a link or credible reference.
  • For rights or licensing notices, please specify your official relationship (publisher, composer, lyricist, recording label) and the target song ID.

Response Times

Our editorial team reviews submissions within 1–2 business days. Urgent copyright takedowns can also be filed directly via the instant notice modal on any song's detail page.
