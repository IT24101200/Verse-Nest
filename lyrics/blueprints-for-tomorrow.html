Song meaning

What does “Blueprints for Tomorrow” mean?

This is a song about ambition without perfectionism. The blueprint is not a finished building: it is a plan that can be revised while life is happening.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

The lyrics repeat building-related images—lines, doors, foundations, bricks—to make the central metaphor easy to remember.
