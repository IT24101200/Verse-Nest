At the midnight grocery store Under buzzing ceiling lights I was choosing between noodles After working through the night There was rain across the windows There were empty aisles of blue And the cashier asked me softly How my evening had been through

Song meaning

What does “Midnight Grocery Store” mean?

A late-night supermarket becomes the setting for a song about ordinary kindness. The cashier does not fix the narrator’s problems, but the brief connection changes how the night feels.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

The small drawing on the bag gives the story a memorable visual turning point. Concrete scenes are especially useful in narrative songwriting.
