Song meaning

What does “Letters to the Sea” mean?

The letter is never sent. Writing it helps the narrator understand their feelings, showing that closure can sometimes come from within.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

The lyrics use the sea as an image of change. A calm chorus balances the longer, detail-rich verses.
