Finding little signs of happiness after a difficult season.

Song meaning

What does “After the Rain” mean?

The song avoids promising that hard days never return. Instead, hope comes from simple actions such as making tea, calling family, and noticing nature.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

Weather is the main image, but the second verse moves into real actions so the message feels practical, not just poetic.
