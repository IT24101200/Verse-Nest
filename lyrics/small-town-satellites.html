Small Town Satellites

Remembering friends who moved to different places.

Verse 1

We learned the road by every pothole Knew the bakery closed at nine Watched the buses leave the corner Swore we'd catch one down the line Now your window faces mountains Mine reflects a river street Different skies above our rooftops Same old rhythm underneath

Chorus

Small town satellites at night Little signals crossing light If you're lonely where you are Send a message through the dark Every mile can stretch the view But it cannot cancel you We are scattered, not erased Holding on to time and place

Song meaning

What does “Small Town Satellites” mean?

The satellite image describes long-distance friendship: communication takes effort, yet a simple message can close emotional distance.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

Specific town details create authenticity. The repeated contrast between different views and shared memories gives the song its emotional pull.
