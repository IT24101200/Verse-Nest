Paper Planets

A gentle love song for two people who dream together.

Verse 1

We drew a planet on a napkin In a kitchen after two Made the oceans out of coffee Made the northern lights from blue You said gravity was simple When a hand could hold a hand And the universe felt smaller Than the place where we would stand

Chorus

Let our paper planets turn Let the tiny lanterns burn We don't need a sky of gold Just a story we can hold If the stars forget to shine Draw another next to mine Let our little world be true I can make a home with you

Verse 2

We kept our wishes in a drawer With the bills we had to pay Found a galaxy of reasons Just to choose each other daily Nothing here is made of silver Nothing waits behind a door But the way you laugh at breakfast Makes me want for nothing more

Bridge

Song meaning

What does “Paper Planets” mean?

Paper planets represent a relationship built through imagination rather than money or grand gestures. The lovers know their plans are fragile, yet they keep choosing each other.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

The verse begins with detailed objects—napkin, coffee, drawer—to make the dreamy chorus feel believable. The bridge acknowledges that love requires repair and effort.
