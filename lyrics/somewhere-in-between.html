Song meaning

What does “Somewhere in Between” mean?

This song is for the period between student life, work, relationships, or any major transition. It argues that uncertainty is a normal part of becoming.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

The first-person language invites listeners to identify with the narrator. The chorus gives permission to keep moving without having every answer.
