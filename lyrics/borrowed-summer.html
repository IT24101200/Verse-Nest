Song meaning

What does “Borrowed Summer” mean?

Borrowed Summer treats temporary love with gratitude rather than anger. The singer chooses to value what was real without demanding that it last forever.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

Warm, sensory details at the beginning provide a contrast with the quiet farewell in the second verse.
