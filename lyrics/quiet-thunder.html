Song meaning

What does “Quiet Thunder” mean?

Quiet Thunder challenges the idea that confidence must look bold or aggressive. Strength can be steady, thoughtful, and private.

Behind the writing

Writing notes

The contrast in the title—quiet and thunder—creates a clear hook. The bridge provides a short statement listeners can remember.
