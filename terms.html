Terms of Use

Conditions and guidelines for browsing, learning, and sharing on VerseNest Originals.

Permitted Educational & Personal Use

VerseNest Originals is offered for personal, educational, and cultural discovery purposes. You are welcome to browse the catalogue, read song backgrounds, study songwriting structures, and share links to our public pages.

Intellectual Property & Restrictions

Original demo lyrics authored for VerseNest remain the property of their respective creators. Songs in the Sinhala Song Catalogue are catalogued for metadata discovery and cultural study; you may not systematically scrape, republish, sell, or commercialize protected lyrics without license from the underlying copyright owners.

Editorial Commentary & Accuracy

Song analysis, historical era tags, and writing advice are editorial commentary and cultural documentation. While we take every effort to verify composer and lyricist credits against primary recording releases, metadata is provided on an educational best-effort basis.

External Links

Where pages link to YouTube, streaming services, or official artist channels, those external platforms maintain their own terms and privacy practices.

Questions & Contact

For questions or terms clarifications, contact us at terms@versenest.org.
