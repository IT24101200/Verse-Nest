About VerseNest Originals

Our purpose, copyright compliance, and cultural mission in Sri Lankan music discovery and lyric craftsmanship.

A Platform for Words and Music Heritage

VerseNest Originals is an independent educational and music discovery platform celebrating Sri Lankan lyricism, cultural music history, and beginner-friendly songwriting craft. We believe every song carries a distinct story shaped by its composer, lyricist, and performer.

What You Will Find Here

Our platform combines two essential components:

  • Sinhala Song Catalogue: A verified database of genuine Sri Lankan music across decades—from golden era icons like W.D. Amaradeva and Milton Mallawarachchi to modern 2020s independent releases.
  • Original Creative Lyrics & Guides: Creative writing samples and practical guides covering song structure, poetic meter, and theme development for aspiring songwriters.

Editorial Ethics & Copyright Adherence

VerseNest strictly honors intellectual property rights. We follow a strict rights-verification policy in full compliance with Google AdSense guidelines and copyright law:

  • Full lyrics are published only where authorized by rights owners or confirmed in the public domain.
  • For copyrighted commercial recordings, we provide verified metadata, editorial context, and official streaming links rather than unauthorized song transcripts.
  • We operate an active Notice & Takedown mechanism for rights holders with immediate resolution.

Academic & Student Project Background

This platform was developed as a clean, high-performance web engineering demonstration by a team of Sri Lankan undergraduate students, combining FastAPI, modern responsive HTML5/CSS3, and persistent SQLite database architecture.
